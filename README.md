<h2 align="center">Key Features</h2>

- **Interactive Camera Practice**: Put your knowledge to the test! Neumá uses advanced technology to read your hand gestures in real-time through your camera. It gives you instant feedback on whether your signs are correct.
- **Gamified Learning Journey**: Embark on an exciting adventure map! Unlock new levels, earn XP (Experience Points), and collect Coins as you progress through interactive quizzes and practical challenges.
- **Leaderboard**: Challenge your friends and see how you stack up against other learners. Show off your collected alphabets and total XP to the world!
- **Bilingual Support**: Learn comfortably in either English or Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Personalized Experience**: Choose your own avatar, state your learning goals, and experience a journey tailored just for you.

<h2 align="center">How it works</h2>

1. **Set Up Your Profile**: Create your account for free and log in to your account.
2. **Follow the Map**: Start from the basics. Each node on the map represents a new lesson or challenge.
3. **Learn and Practice**: Read the instructions, memorize the signs, and take quizzes to test your memory.
4. **Show Your Hands**: Perform the sign in front of your camera, and our system will tell you if you got it right!
5. **Earn Rewards**: Finish the level with high accuracy to earn maximum XP and Coins. 

<h2 align="center">Privacy First</h2>

Your privacy is our top priority. Neumá only uses your camera during the interactive practice sessions to detect your hand gestures. We process this data in real-time, meaning **no video is ever recorded, saved, or uploaded**.
